# HubSpot CRM — Customer Relationship Management Portfolio

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my practical experience using **HubSpot CRM** to manage customer information, organize sales activities, track customer interactions, manage deals, and maintain structured customer relationship workflows.

The project was designed to simulate real-world CRM and customer support operations, with a focus on keeping customer information organized, maintaining accurate records, managing follow-ups, and supporting the customer journey from initial contact through resolution or sales progression.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to demonstrate my ability to:

- Manage and organize customer contact records
- Maintain accurate customer information
- Track customer interactions and activities
- Record customer support requests and issues
- Manage follow-up activities and tasks
- Track sales opportunities and deals
- Monitor deal stages throughout the sales process
- Associate customer activities with relevant deals
- Maintain organized customer communication records
- Support customer retention through timely follow-up
- Use CRM data to improve customer relationship management

---

## HubSpot CRM Activities Demonstrated

### 1. Contact Management

Created and managed customer contact records containing information such as:

- Customer name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Job title
- Contact owner
- Lifecycle stage
- Lead status
- Last contacted date

This demonstrates the ability to maintain structured customer records and quickly access important customer information.

---

### 2. Customer Activity Tracking

Used HubSpot's activity timeline to document and monitor customer interactions, including:

- Emails
- Notes
- Calls
- Tasks
- Follow-up activities
- Customer status changes
- Deal-related activities

Maintaining a detailed activity history makes it easier to understand the customer's journey and provide consistent support.

---

### 3. Customer Support Request Management

Documented customer support issues and requests and connected relevant customer interactions to their records.

Examples of simulated customer issues include:

- Laptop overheating
- Login problems
- Product information requests
- Laptop-related technical issues

The workflow demonstrates how CRM records can be used to maintain visibility over customer issues and their resolution.

---

### 4. Follow-Up Management

Created follow-up tasks to ensure that outstanding customer requests and sales opportunities were not overlooked.

Examples include:

- Following up with customers about purchases
- Calling customers regarding outstanding requests
- Following up on proposals
- Monitoring customers awaiting responses
- Scheduling future customer interactions

This demonstrates the importance of proactive communication and consistent follow-up in customer relationship management.

---

### 5. Deal Management

Created and managed sales opportunities within HubSpot CRM.

Examples of simulated deals include:

- Technical Support Contract
- HP Laptop Upgrade
- Dell Laptop Purchase
- ABC Redesign of Cloth
- Sales of Bale

Each opportunity was tracked through different stages of the sales process.

---

### 6. Sales Pipeline Tracking

Used deal stages to monitor the progress of opportunities through the sales pipeline.

Examples of stages demonstrated include:

- Appointment Scheduled
- Qualified To Buy
- Presentation Scheduled
- Closed Won

This provides visibility into the status of sales opportunities and helps ensure that follow-up actions are completed at the appropriate stage.

---

### 7. Customer Relationship Management

Used customer information, activities, notes, emails, tasks, and deals together to create a more complete view of each customer.

This demonstrates how CRM systems can help support:

- Customer communication
- Sales coordination
- Customer support
- Follow-up management
- Relationship building
- Retention
- Opportunity tracking

---

## Practical CRM Workflow

The project follows a structured customer management workflow:

```text
Customer Contact
       ↓
Contact Record Created
       ↓
Customer Information Captured
       ↓
Customer Request / Opportunity Identified
       ↓
Interaction Recorded
       ↓
Task or Follow-Up Created
       ↓
Deal / Support Activity Tracked
       ↓
Customer Follow-Up
       ↓
Issue Resolved / Opportunity Progressed
       ↓
Customer Relationship Maintained
