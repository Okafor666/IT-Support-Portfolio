# Freshdesk Customer Support & Ticket Management

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates practical experience using **Freshdesk** to manage customer support requests, document customer interactions, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain an organized ticket-resolution workflow.

The project covers realistic customer service and technical support scenarios, including laptop problems, login issues, refund requests, product information requests, technical issues, and troubleshooting cases.

The objective was to demonstrate how a customer support professional can use a help desk platform to:

- Receive and organize customer support requests
- Understand and categorize customer issues
- Communicate professionally with customers
- Investigate and troubleshoot technical problems
- Provide clear resolutions and next steps
- Document internal support activities
- Track ticket status and follow-up requirements
- Maintain accurate customer and ticket information


## Tools & Technologies

- **Freshdesk** — Customer support and ticket management
- **Email Support** — Customer communication and follow-up
- **Phone Support** — Handling customer-reported issues
- **Ticket Management** — Tracking, prioritizing, and resolving support requests
- **Internal Notes** — Documenting troubleshooting and resolution activities

# Ticket Scenarios

## 1. Technical Issue — Apple PC Not Charging

**Scenario:**  
A customer reported an urgent issue with a newly purchased Apple PC that was not charging. The customer needed the computer to complete an important task for their company.

**Support Approach:**
- Reviewed the customer's reported issue.
- Identified the request as urgent.
- Assessed the reported charging problem.
- Communicated professionally with the customer.
- Documented the ticket for appropriate follow-up.

**Skills Demonstrated:**

# Customer Communication Approach

Throughout the project, customer communication was handled using a professional and customer-focused approach.

Key principles demonstrated include:

- Acknowledge the customer's concern.
- Use the customer's name when appropriate.
- Clearly understand the reported problem.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Provide concise and actionable information.
- Set appropriate expectations.
- Maintain a respectful tone, particularly when handling complaints.
- Confirm or document resolution.
- Encourage customers to report unresolved or recurring issues.
